Roadmap to the platinum

Three stages, in order. Finish the story, then the 100% checklist, then the stragglers — and Vice Got a Price! is yours. About 200–300 hours in total, by the cited guides.

⚠ One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots, and do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1. Click Stage 1 below to see it.

There are no online trophies: all 55 are single-player and offline, so this filter is empty by design.
